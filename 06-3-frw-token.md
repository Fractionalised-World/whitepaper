# 3. FRW Token

The FRW token is a digital utility token built on BNB Chain and designed to enable interaction with the Fractionalised World ecosystem. The token functions primarily within the protocol’s staking framework and emission mechanics, which are structured to support ongoing ecosystem participation and controlled token distribution over time.

The token economy is designed with an emphasis on predictable emissions, transparent distribution mechanics and long-term ecosystem sustainability.

By utilising structured token allocation parameters rather than percentage-based yield mechanisms, the protocol aims to encourage continued participation while reducing incentives associated with short-term speculative behaviour.

## 3.1. Token Details

The FRW token has a maximum supply of 10,000,000,000 (10 billion) tokens.

The native deployment chain is BNB Chain, with the potential for future multi-chain deployment as the ecosystem evolves. Multi-chain expansion may enable broader accessibility across different blockchain networks and digital asset communities.

The FRW token is intended to be accepted and redeemable solely within the Fractionalised World ecosystem and its associated protocol mechanics.

The FRW token is not designed to function as an asset-referenced token or e-money token as defined under Regulation EU 2023/1114 on Markets in Crypto-Assets.

## 3.2. Supply and Staking Framework

The majority of the FRW token supply, 9,975,000,000 tokens (99.75%), is held within the staking distribution contract. This allocation forms the basis of the ecosystem's structured token distribution framework.

By linking token distribution to protocol participation, the emission model is intended to support gradual token circulation while maintaining transparency regarding long-term distribution parameters.

At ecosystem launch an initial circulating supply of 25,000,000 FRW tokens will be available. These tokens are allocated across several operational and ecosystem categorie with final wallet allocations determined prior to launch.

## 3.3. Staking Participation Framework

The FRW staking framework introduces a tiered participation structure. Participants who choose to stake FRW tokens may interact with five participation tiers that correspond to structured daily token allocation parameters.

Illustrative allocation levels within the current framework range from approximately 0.1 FRW per day at Tier 1 up to 5 FRW per day at Tier 5 under the maximum protocol configuration.

These figures are indicative only and may vary depending on ecosystem participation levels and protocol configuration.

Themed NFTs may interact with the staking framework by applying allocation multipliers and providing eligibility for additional entries within the ecosystem’s promotional reward event mechanism.

All allocation figures are illustrative and do not represent guaranteed outcomes.

## 3.4. Token Distribution Emission Model

The Fractionalised World emission model operates through a structured token distribution framework designed to support long-term ecosystem sustainability.

Unlike many DeFi protocols that rely on percentage-based yield mechanisms, the FRW emission model uses predetermined allocation parameters tied to active participation within the ecosystem. This approach prioritises transparency and predictability over dynamic yield calculations and is designed to reduce the inflationary pressure often associated with percentage-based distribution systems.

The emission model operates through three primary participation layers, each of which contributes to a participant's overall allocation parameters within the protocol:

**Access NFT Participation Layer**

Each Access NFT that is active within the protocol may interact with the emission framework and contribute a base allocation of up to 1 FRW per day under the current protocol configuration.

**Staking Participation Layer**

Participants who choose to stake FRW tokens may interact with the tiered staking framework. The protocol currently supports five participation tiers, with higher tiers corresponding to larger daily allocation parameters. Staking is designed to encourage continued engagement within the ecosystem rather than short-term token rotation.

**Themed NFT Multiplier Layer**

Themed NFTs may apply protocol allocation multipliers to the combined base generated through Access NFT participation and staking. Multipliers may reach up to 6x under the maximum protocol configuration when the permitted maximum number of Themed NFTs is held. This layer is designed to reward broader ecosystem engagement and create a gamified participation experience.

These three layers are designed to work together to incentivise deeper ecosystem participation while maintaining predictable and transparent distribution mechanics. No single layer operates in isolation, and the interaction between layers is intended to encourage participants to engage with multiple aspects of the protocol.

Actual allocations may vary based on ecosystem participation levels and protocol configuration.

### 3.4.1. Staking Tier Structure

The staking participation tiers currently operate under the following illustrative framework:

- Tier 1: 100 – 499 FRW staked
- Maximum allocation parameter: up to 0.1 FRW per day
- Tier 2: 500 – 4,999 FRW staked
- Maximum allocation parameter: up to 0.5 FRW per day
- Tier 3: 5,000 – 24,999 FRW staked
- Maximum allocation parameter: up to 1 FRW per day
- Tier 4: 25,000 – 49,999 FRW staked
- Maximum allocation parameter: up to 2.5 FRW per day
- Tier 5: 50,000+ FRW staked
- Maximum allocation parameter: up to 5 FRW per day
Each tier's base allocation may combine with the Access NFT participation layer before Themed NFT multipliers are applied. The tiered structure is designed to reflect varying levels of protocol engagement rather than to function as a financial return mechanism of any kind.

All tier parameters are illustrative and may be modified as the ecosystem evolves.

The following scenario illustrates the maximum allocation configuration for a participant interacting with all available protocol layers. It is provided solely to demonstrate how the three participation layers interact under maximum protocol conditions and does not represent a typical participation outcome.

A participant holding:

- 5 Themed NFTs
- 50,000 FRW staked
may interact with the emission framework under the following illustrative parameters:

- Access NFT allocation: up to 1 FRW/day

This figure represents the theoretical upper boundary of the protocol under current parameters. It is provided for structural illustration only. Actual allocations may vary significantly based on protocol configuration, participation levels and ecosystem conditions.

### 3.4.3. Ecosystem Maximum Emission Scenario

The ecosystem supports a maximum supply of 20,000 Access NFTs. Under a purely illustrative scenario in which all Access NFTs are active, all participants operate at the highest staking tier and each wallet holds the maximum number of Themed NFTs, the theoretical maximum daily emission across the entire ecosystem would be 720,000 FRW.

This scenario represents the absolute upper boundary of the emission framework and is considered unlikely to occur under typical participation conditions. Actual emissions are expected to vary significantly across participants and over time.

### 3.4.4. Distribution Contract Sustainability

The staking distribution contract holds 9,975,000,000 FRW tokens, representing 99.75% of the total token supply. This allocation forms the structural basis of the ecosystem's long-term distribution framework.

Under the maximum emission scenario described above, the contract would hold sufficient token reserves to sustain the emission framework for approximately 37.9 years. Given that full ecosystem participation at maximum configuration is unlikely to occur continuously, the practical sustainability window is expected to extend significantly beyond this illustrative figure.

### 3.4.5. Circulating Supply and Distribution Mechanics

At launch, the circulating supply of 25,000,000 FRW represents 0.25% of the total token supply. The substantial majority of tokens remain locked within the staking distribution contract and are distributed gradually through ecosystem participation mechanics over time.

This phased distribution model is intended to maintain long-term supply transparency while aligning token circulation with active protocol participation rather than speculative demand.

## 3.6. Seed Participation Round

A total of 6,000,000 FRW tokens are reserved for early ecosystem participants who supported the initial development phase of the platform in exchange for token allocations subject to the staged release schedule described below.

These tokens are subject to a six-month staged release schedule, with 1,000,000 FRW unlocking per month following launch.

The staged release schedule is a technical distribution parameter designed to support orderly token distribution and reduce potential market disruption. It does not constitute a vesting arrangement, investment return schedule or any form of financial instrument.

Participation in this allocation does not provide governance rights, equity ownership, revenue share or entitlement to financial returns.
