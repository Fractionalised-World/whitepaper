# 2. NFTs

## 2.1. Access NFTs: Big Bang Series

The Big Bang Access NFT (hereafter referred to as the "BBA NFT") is the first iteration of the Access NFT gateway to the Fractionalised World ecosystem. Serving as the initial access mechanism, it provides users with the ability to interact with the platform and its available features. The BBA NFT enables interaction with the ecosystem’s protocol mechanics, including participation in the protocol’s token distribution framework, staking functionality and access to future ecosystem developments as they are introduced.

Additionally, the Access NFT functions as an entry mechanism for the platform's periodic promotional reward event. Holding an Access NFT may provide eligibility to participate in promotional reward events conducted within the ecosystem, subject to applicable rules and availability. As the foundational access component of the Fractionalised World experience, the BBA NFT is designed to facilitate user interaction and engagement within the platform as it evolves over time.

Note: Subsequent iterations of the Access NFT, including future themed collections, may be introduced in later phases of the ecosystem as determined by the platform.

## 2.2. Access NFTs Tiered Pricing Structure

The distribution of Access NFTs is organised through a phased release structure designed to gradually introduce participants to the Fractionalised World ecosystem. Different phases provide varying bundle compositions while maintaining consistent access to the platform’s core functionality. One exception to this structure is the Whale Bundle: which represents a limited allocation intended for participants seeking larger ecosystem allocations.

### 2.2.1. Whitelist Bundle

The series begins with the Whitelist Bundle (QTY 500), designed to provide early participants with initial access to the Fractionalised World ecosystem. Participants gain entry through the BBA NFT which serves as the primary access mechanism to the platform.

In addition to the BBA NFT, participants in the Whitelist Bundle receive one Themed NFT. Participants will also receive an allocation of 1,000 FRW tokens which may be distributed at the time of ecosystem launch as part of the initial bundle composition.

This bundle composition introduces participants to the ecosystem through a combination of the BBA NFT, a Themed NFT and FRW tokens, allowing them to interact with the platform’s features and mechanics as they become available.

Further information about Themed NFTs and FRW tokens, including their design and functionality within the ecosystem is provided in later sections.

### 2.2.2. Pioneer Bundle

Following the completion of the Whitelist Bundle phase, the next release phase known as the Pioneer Bundle will introduce an additional 1,990 Access NFTs.

Each Pioneer Bundle includes one BBA NFT and one Themed NFT. Participants will also receive an allocation of 500 FRW tokens which may be distributed at the time of ecosystem launch as part of the bundle composition.

This phased release structure allows additional participants to access the Fractionalised World ecosystem while maintaining a consistent framework for ecosystem engagement.

Further information about Themed NFTs and FRW tokens, including their design and functionality within the ecosystem is provided in later sections.

### 2.2.3. Whale Bundle

Fractionalised World may make available a limited Whale Bundle intended for a small number of participants seeking larger ecosystem allocations. This bundle is strictly limited to 10 allocations.

In this bundle composition, the BBA NFT is provided alongside a larger allocation of FRW tokens and multiple Themed NFTs. The bundle includes one BBA NFT, 50,000 FRW tokens and five Themed NFTs.

The Whale Bundle is structured to allow participants who wish to interact with the ecosystem at a larger scale to obtain the relevant digital assets required for participation within the platform.

Further information about Themed NFTs and FRW tokens, including their design and functionality within the ecosystem is provided in later sections.

## 2.3. Access NFTs Distribution Mechanics

Each Big Bang Access NFT is designed to interact with the protocol’s emission framework when staked, which may result in the allocation of up to 1 FRW per day under the current protocol configuration, assuming continuous participation and unchanged protocol parameters. Emissions are structured and do not compound, supporting a transparent and predictable token distribution model.

Fractionalised World employs a structured emission model rather than a percentage-based return on capital. This design reflects an emphasis on protocol participation, emission control and economic sustainability rather than yield-based incentives.

### 2.3.1. Transparent Emissions and Supply Control

A structured emission allocation per NFT enables the protocol to estimate FRW token distribution over time. Unlike percentage-based yield models which scale dynamically with capital inflows, structured emissions establish a predictable issuance framework that may reduce uncertainty and assist in managing token supply dynamics.

### 2.3.2. Maintaining Accessible Participation

The Access NFT is designed primarily as a mechanism for interaction with the Fractionalised World platform. Structured emissions are intended to support equitable participation within the protocol. Percentage-based yield models may concentrate allocations among larger capital participants, whereas structured emissions seek to maintain balanced participation across the ecosystem.

### 2.3.3. Limiting Short-Term Incentive Structure

Percentage-based yield structures can encourage short-term participation focused on rapid capital rotation. Structured emissions are designed to support ongoing engagement within the ecosystem rather than short-term optimisation of token distributions.

### 2.3.4. Regulatory Considerations

The structured emission framework is designed to support the functional utility of Access NFTs within the ecosystem. The mechanism is intended to facilitate protocol participation rather than operate as a financial instrument, interest-bearing product, or investment contract.

### 2.3.5. Long-Term Ecosystem Sustainability

Percentage-based yield mechanisms can create token supply pressures as participation scales. Structured emissions aim to prioritise the long-term sustainability of the ecosystem by establishing a predictable distribution model aligned with platform participation.

## 2.4. Access NFTs and the Promotional Reward Event

The ecosystem includes a periodic promotional reward event mechanism, referred to within this section as the promotional reward event, intended solely as a community engagement feature within the platform.

### 2.4.1. Funding Sources

The promotional reward event contract receives allocations at the time of NFT mint transactions through predefined routing parameters:

- Big Bang Access NFTs: A portion of the mint transaction may be allocated to the
promotional reward event smart contract.

- Themed NFTs: A portion of the mint transaction may also be allocated to the
promotional reward event smart contract.

- These transfers occur automatically as part of the mint transaction logic.
### 2.4.2. Distribution Schedule

Standard Promotional Event: Executed periodically with a target distribution level that may depend on the balance available within the promotional reward event contract.

Accumulated Promotional Event: Executed periodically using accumulated funds that have not previously been distributed.

Distribution timing and amounts may vary depending on available contract balances and protocol configuration.

### 2.4.3. Winner Selection

Eligible participants may be selected through a verifiable randomness mechanism designed to ensure fairness and transparency. Industry-standard randomness solutions may be used to support auditability.

Eligibility parameters may evolve over time as the protocol develops.

### 2.4.4. Payout Options

Selected participants may be offered one of several settlement options depending on availability and jurisdiction:

- Cash Settlement: On-chain stablecoin transfer or equivalent payout mechanism
where permitted.

- Asset Settlement: Selection from available digital or physical items subject to regional
availability and logistics.

- Extended Event Entry: Conversion of the periodic allocation into an entry for a larger
promotional reward event.

Asset and settlement availability may depend on jurisdiction, logistics constraints and supplier availability.

### 2.4.5. Risk and Compliance

The promotional reward event mechanism is intended solely as a community engagement feature within the ecosystem. Participation does not provide any ownership rights, profit-sharing rights, or entitlement to financial returns.

Fractionalised World may modify eligibility rules, distribution parameters, timing, and operational mechanics as required to support platform development and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Participants remain solely responsible for ensuring that their participation complies with the laws and regulations applicable in their jurisdiction.

## 2.5. Allocation of Access NFT Mint Proceeds

Funds generated through the minting of Access NFTs may be allocated across several operational categories intended to support the development and operation of the Fractionalised World ecosystem.

A portion of mint transaction proceeds may be routed to the promotional reward event smart contract to support community engagement initiatives conducted within the ecosystem.

A portion of funds may also be allocated toward founder compensation in recognition of the time, expertise and resources required to design, build and maintain the platform.

Marketing initiatives may receive allocations intended to support community growth, ecosystem awareness and strategic partnerships.

Funds may also be directed toward technical development and platform maintenance to ensure the continued reliability, security and functionality of the ecosystem.

A strategic reserve may be maintained to allow the platform to respond to future opportunities, partnerships or ecosystem development initiatives.

Additional allocations may be used for operational expenses, administrative costs and legal or regulatory compliance requirements associated with maintaining the platform.

These allocation categories are indicative in nature and may evolve as the platform develops. Fractionalised World reserves the right to modify allocation priorities as necessary to support the ongoing development and sustainability of the ecosystem

## 2.6. Themed NFTs

Themed NFTs represent participation in distinct chapters associated with historical eras, economic sectors or conceptual stages of global development within the Fractionalised World ecosystem.

Holding Themed NFTs enables additional interaction with certain protocol mechanics, including adjustments to token distribution multipliers within the structured emission framework and eligibility for additional entries into ecosystem promotional reward events.

### 2.6.1. Eligibility

Eligibility to mint Themed NFTs is linked to the holding of a BBA NFT. Eligible participants may mint Themed NFTs during designated mint periods.

The protocol currently anticipates a maximum mint limit of five Themed NFTs per Access NFT, although these parameters may evolve as the ecosystem develops.

### 2.6.2. Themed NFT Distribution Mechanics

Themed NFTs may apply protocol allocation multipliers to a participant's combined emission base which includes both the Access NFT participation layer and the staking participation layer. The combined multiplier may reach up to 6x under the maximum protocol configuration when the permitted maximum of five Themed NFTs is held. For a full illustration of how multipliers interact with the combined emission base, refer to Section 3.4 Token Distribution Emission Model.

Holding Themed NFTs may also provide additional eligibility entries for the ecosystem's periodic promotional reward event mechanism.

Multipliers and eligibility parameters operate within the structured emission framework and do not constitute guarantees of specific token amounts. Actual token allocations may vary based on protocol configuration, participation levels and ecosystem conditions.

All figures presented are illustrative and subject to change.

### 2.6.3. Allocation of Themed NFT Mint Proceeds

Funds generated through the minting of Themed NFTs may be allocated across several operational categories intended to support the ecosystem.

#### 2.6.3.1. Administrative and Operational Expenses

A portion of proceeds may be allocated toward administrative costs, operational overhead, platform development and regulatory compliance.

#### 2.6.3.2. Community Engagement Initiatives

A portion of mint transaction proceeds may be routed to the promotional reward event smart contract or other ecosystem engagement initiatives.

#### 2.6.3.3. Platform Capital Deployment

A portion of funds may be allocated by Fractionalised World as part of its independent business strategy. Such capital may be deployed into sectors or opportunities identified by the company from time to time.

These activities are conducted independently of ecosystem participation.

#### 2.6.3.4. Important Notice Regarding Capital Deployment

Themed NFT holders do not obtain ownership rights, equity interests, revenue participation, or legal claims over any assets, ventures or activities undertaken by Fractionalised World or its affiliated entities.

The acquisition or holding of a Themed NFT does not constitute an investment in any underlying asset, business or venture.

Participation in the ecosystem does not provide any entitlement to profits, revenue, dividends or financial returns generated by the company or its affiliated entities.

In the event that Fractionalised World or any affiliated entity ceases operations, Themed NFT holders and ecosystem participants shall have no claim or recourse against the assets or operations of the company.

#### 2.6.3.5. Protocol Utility

The primary functional utility associated with Themed NFTs is interaction with the ecosystem’s structured token emission framework and eligibility for community engagement initiatives such as promotional reward events.

The FRW token functions as a utility token within the ecosystem and is distributed through structured protocol emissions based on participation. It does not represent an investment contract, profit-sharing mechanism, or financial yield instrument.
