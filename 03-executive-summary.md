# Executive Summary

Fractionalised World (FRW) is a next-generation DeFi ecosystem that connects blockchain technology with real-world economic activity through its proprietary Digital Fusions mechanism.

Unlike traditional DeFi protocols that rely purely on speculative tokenomics, Fractionalised World is designed to create a structured and sustainable ecosystem grounded in real-world economic activity while maintaining the accessibility and engagement of a blockchain-based participation framework.

Participants purchase Access NFTs and Themed NFTs to engage with the ecosystem which may enable interaction with the protocol's structured token distribution mechanics and eligibility for community engagement events.

The Access NFT serves as the essential entry point to the platform, enabling interaction with the protocol's token distribution framework, staking participation tiers and the ecosystem's promotional reward events.

Themed NFTs enhance this experience by applying protocol allocation multipliers of up to 6x to a participant's combined emission base and granting additional eligibility entries into promotional reward events, creating a gamified and engaging ecosystem.

The FRW token is built on BNB Chain with a maximum supply of 10,000,000,000 tokens. 99.75% of supply is locked within the staking distribution contract ensuring controlled emissions and long-term supply stability. By focusing on structured token distribution parameters rather than percentage-based yield mechanisms, FRW is designed to support sustainable long-term participation while minimising incentives associated with short-term speculative behaviour.

Fractionalised World has sought independent legal guidance in connection with the design of its ecosystem. Regulatory compliance and long-term ecosystem sustainability are at the core of every design decision within Fractionalised World.
