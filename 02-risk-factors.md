# RISK FACTORS

Participation in the FRW ecosystem involves significant risk. Prospective participants should carefully consider all of the following risk factors before engaging with the platform.

### Token Value Risk

The FRW token is a digital asset whose value may fluctuate significantly and without warning. There is no guarantee that the token will retain any particular value after issuance. Participants may lose some or all of the value attributed to their tokens. The token has no guaranteed redemption value and is not backed by fiat currency or any tangible asset.

### Token Distribution Risk

All token allocation figures, emission parameters and participation scenarios described in this whitepaper are illustrative and represent theoretical maximum configurations under current protocol conditions. Actual token allocations may vary based on ecosystem participation levels, protocol configuration and smart contract performance. Token distributions within the ecosystem do not constitute a financial return, yield, income or profit of any kind. The FRW token is a utility token intended solely for interaction with the Fractionalised World protocol.

### Smart Contract Risk

The ecosystem operates through smart contracts deployed on Ethereum. While designed to operate autonomously and transparently, smart contracts are subject to technical vulnerabilities, bugs and exploits. Fractionalised World may conduct third-party security audits prior to launch; however no audit can guarantee complete security and the occurrence of a vulnerability could result in total loss of funds.

### Regulatory Risk

The regulatory treatment of digital assets, NFTs and DeFi protocols varies significantly across jurisdictions and continues to evolve rapidly. Changes in applicable laws or regulations could adversely affect the operation of the platform, the value of the FRW token or the ability of participants to access the ecosystem. Fractionalised World may be required to modify, suspend or terminate aspects of the ecosystem in response to regulatory developments without prior notice.

### Platform Risk

Fractionalised World is a new platform currently in development. There is no guarantee that the platform will launch as planned or achieve the level of adoption necessary to sustain the ecosystem long term. Development timelines may be subject to change.

### Liquidity Risk

There is no guarantee that a liquid market for the FRW token will develop or be maintained after launch. Participants may find it difficult to sell or exchange their tokens at a price reflective of their perceived value. NFTs may have no secondary market.

### Third Party Risk

The ecosystem relies on third party service providers including centralised and decentralised exchanges, oracle providers and infrastructure partners. The failure or underperformance of any third party could adversely affect the platform.

### Capital Allocation Risk

Fractionalised World Group allocates capital as part of its own independent business strategy. The performance of these capital allocations is not guaranteed. Participants have no ownership stake, legal interest or proprietary claim over any assets held, managed or operated by Fractionalised World Group or any entity within the group. The performance of any capital allocation does not create any obligation or liability toward ecosystem participants. The existence of a Digital Fusion associated with any such activity does not alter this separation or create economic rights for ecosystem participants.

### No Recourse

In the event that Fractionalised World or any entity within the Fractionalised World Group ceases operations for any reason, ecosystem participants shall have no claim, recourse or entitlement over any assets, funds or operations of any entity within the group. The purchase of any NFT or token confers no ownership rights, creditor rights or any other legal interest in Fractionalised World or any associated entity.

### Community Engagement Initiative Risk

Community engagement initiatives are intended to support participation and engagement within the ecosystem. Individual initiatives may include quests, testing programmes, educational activities, community contributions, campaigns, airdrops or other engagement activities. Eligibility, rewards and participation requirements may vary between initiatives and jurisdictions. Fractionalised World reserves the right to modify, restrict or discontinue any initiative at any time. Participants are responsible for ensuring that their participation complies with applicable laws in their jurisdiction.
